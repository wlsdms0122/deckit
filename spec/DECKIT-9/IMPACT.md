# What DECKIT-9 changes

| DECKIT | sentence | change | class |
|---|---|---|---|
| DECKIT-2 | *interfaces*: "What providing and using come to (an interface's owner, the connections, a major that does not match) is the connections' DECKIT." | what providing and using come to is the interfaces' DECKIT's, and what a connection is links [the connections](../DECKIT-9/SPEC.md) | supplement |
| DECKIT-2 | Interactions: "What `provides`, `uses` and `contributes` come to: the connections' DECKIT." | the same split: the interfaces' DECKIT, and [the connections](../DECKIT-9/SPEC.md) for what a connection is | supplement |
| DECKIT-3 | Interactions: "The requests `deckit.reach-mods` guards, reaching a mod by its id, and what a mod providing an interface is told of the permissions its client is granted: the connections' DECKIT." | links [the connections](../DECKIT-9/SPEC.md), which fix the one request it guards, `dependencies.reach`, and what every service is handed of its client; made in place, DECKIT-3 being a draft | supplement |
| DECKIT-3 | Notes: "That a host names the guards of its own interfaces' requests waits on the connections' DECKIT, which gives a host interfaces of its own." | waits on the interfaces' DECKIT; made in place | supplement |
| DECKIT-5 | *What launches a mod*: "A lifecycle that waits for the mod, as the connections' DECKIT says, unless the mod is at the relaunch limit." | "A lifecycle that reaches the mod, as [the connections](../DECKIT-9/SPEC.md) say.": the relaunch limit stays one of the refusals there, beside a latest run that failed to launch, so the condition is stated in one place; made in place, DECKIT-5 being a draft | supplement |
| DECKIT-5 | Interactions: "A lifecycle that waits for a mod, and what a run that ends does to its connections: the connections' DECKIT." | links [the connections](../DECKIT-9/SPEC.md); made in place | supplement |
| DECKIT-5 | Notes: "A lifecycle that waits for a mod waits on the connections' DECKIT." | removed: the cases of [the connections](../DECKIT-9/SPEC.md) hold a mod launched as it is reached; made in place | supplement |
| DECKIT-6 | Interactions: "What each lifecycle is connected to as it starts, and what a lifecycle that ends does to its connections: the connections' DECKIT." | split: what a lifecycle that ends does to its connections and the exports a lifecycle opens for the mod's id link [the connections](../DECKIT-9/SPEC.md); what a lifecycle is connected to as it starts is the interfaces' DECKIT's; made in place, DECKIT-6 being a draft | supplement |
| DECKIT-7 | *The handle*, the row `mods.pin(interface, mod)`: "the connections' DECKIT" | the interfaces' DECKIT; made in place, DECKIT-7 being a draft | supplement |
| DECKIT-7 | *The handle*, the row `exports`, `dependencies`: "what the mod provides, and what it is connected to … the connections' DECKIT" | split in three rows: `exports.register(exports)`, and `dependencies.reach(id)` with `dependencies.release(id)`, `reach` guarded by `deckit.reach-mods`, link [the connections](../DECKIT-9/SPEC.md); the members of an interface, `exports.register(interface, exports)`, `exports.contributions(interface)`, `dependencies.onConnect` and `dependencies.onRefuse`, are the interfaces' DECKIT's; made in place | supplement |

No case of DECKIT-1 to DECKIT-8 changes its answer: no case of theirs reaches a mod by its id or opens exports.
