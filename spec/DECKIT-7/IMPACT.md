The sentences that leave a mod quitting itself (DECKIT-5, *What ends a run* and Interactions) and the addresses a mod hands the host to open (DECKIT-1, the row "the addresses to open outside the app", the look `opened` and Interactions) to the handle's DECKIT are not changed here: the handle leaves both to the DECKITs that state them.
